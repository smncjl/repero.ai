# AI transparency page

The public pages at `/en/trust/ai` and `/fr/trust/ai` explain how Repero uses AI, where human review remains necessary, and how the product approaches providers, data, limitations, and content provenance.

The marketing site currently supports English and French only. Add another locale only when there is a clear acquisition or user need and its marketing, policy, visual, and product content can be maintained to the same standard.

When maintaining these pages:

- do not claim AI Act certification or blanket compliance;
- review the content when a provider, processing arrangement, or material product policy changes;
- describe only capabilities available in the product today and never present a planned feature as already deployed;
- keep details aligned with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service rather than duplicating them.
