# Repero brand

## Current state

- **Original logo:** historical/reference. The unchanged production source remains at `public/brand/repero-icon.svg`; `logo/original/repero-icon-original.svg` is its reference copy.
- **Flat mark:** current candidate at `logo/flat/repero-mark-flat.svg`.
- **Wordmark:** experimental, not final, at `wordmark/repero-wordmark-experimental.svg`.
- **Brand tokens:** proposal, not yet applied to production, at `audit/repero-brand-tokens.proposed.json`.

## Rules

- No new R geometry experiments.
- No compass.
- No duplicated R.
- No random logo variants.
- No production replacement without explicit approval.

The flat candidate preserves the recognizable rounded Repero R silhouette. Its structure is `#050A1C`; its detached locator dot is `#22D3EE`. The wordmark is exactly `[R]epero AI`: the flat R is the only R.
