# Repero AI LinkedIn template notes

These rules derive from the two introduction layouts. Keep the current official wordmark file unchanged and place it as the footer signature; do not add a separate logo or any additional wordmark treatment.

## Canvas and structure

- Canvas: `1200 × 1200 px`.
- Outer margin: `56 px`; inner content margin: `112 px` for the centered layout and `136 px` for the left-aligned layout.
- Background: night ink (`#050A1C`) with one rounded inner panel (`#0B1531`, `56 px` radius, `#334155` 2 px stroke).
- Keep one restrained cyan rule (`#22D3EE`, `64 × 4 px`) above the headline. Do not add decorative imagery, gradients, screens, devices, or CTA controls.

## Headline

- Typeface: Space Grotesk, matching the current brand visual language.
- Centered layout: 58 px, 700 weight, `-2.6 px` tracking, centered within a maximum text width of 976 px.
- Left-aligned layout: 56 px, 700 weight, `-2.5 px` tracking, aligned to the 136 px content edge; retain a maximum text width of 928 px.
- Use one short message only. Maintain at least 140 px of clear space between the final headline line and the footer divider.

## Footer signature

- Divider: `976 × 2 px`, positioned at `x=112`, `y=820`.
- Place `repero-wordmark-white-cyan.svg` as-is on the dark LinkedIn panel, without cropping, recolouring, redrawing, or pairing it with a separate mark. Use `repero-wordmark-transparent.svg` only on light backgrounds. Direct raster exports are used only so the LinkedIn SVGs remain reliable for PNG export.
- The source wordmark has a square `1080 × 1080` canvas. Render it at `600 × 600 px` so its existing pale signature area and internal artwork retain their proportions.
- Centered placement: `x=300`, `y=648`; left placement: `x=80`, `y=648`.
- URL: `repero.ai` only; 26 px Space Grotesk Medium in `#94A3B8`, at `y=1080`. Center it for the centered layout and align it to `x=136` for the left layout. Never use `app.repero.ai`.

## Reuse rules

- Choose either the centered or left-aligned composition; do not create additional layout families without approval.
- Preserve the margins, panel, headline scale, footer divider, wordmark treatment, and URL placement.
- The visual supports the LinkedIn copy. Do not add subtitles that restate the post, slogans, feature lists, or a fake CTA.
