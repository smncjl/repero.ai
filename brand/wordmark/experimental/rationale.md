# Experimental wordmark and LinkedIn visual rationale

Status: experimental only. These files do not change production UI, token values, or the canonical logo.

## Wordmark selection

The comparison tests Space Grotesk first, as required by the approved audit and current website implementation. The selected study is A.

- The immutable transparent official R foreground geometry is reproduced verbatim from `public/brand/repero-icon-transparent.svg` for self-contained experimental SVG rendering; it is not redrawn, simplified, or changed. It is the sole R in `Repero AI`; the companion text begins with `epero`.
- `epero` is Space Grotesk Bold, placed 17 units after the R's visible right edge. This preserves word recognition without making the official dot compete with the lowercase `e`.
- The text baseline is 125 in the 190-unit wordmark, while the R's locator dot extends slightly below it. That deliberate offset lets the dot read as an endpoint/locator rather than punctuation on the text baseline.
- `AI` uses Space Grotesk Medium and muted ink to preserve the name as the primary read.

Variant B shows why more separation weakens the word into icon-plus-text. Variant C shows why a lighter `epero` loses too much weight beside the official 44-unit rounded stroke.

## LinkedIn visual

The square uses actual Repero ingredients: ink/slate structure, a 56px soft outer radius, thin cool boundaries, limited sky/cyan accents, and generous negative space. The pale dotted route is intentionally quiet: it echoes organization and retrieval without recreating or extracting the logo's diagonal or dot.

The message is the visual's single idea. Supporting copy is limited to one sentence; there is no app URL. The selected experimental wordmark appears once at the base, with its official R supplying the only R in the wordmark.
